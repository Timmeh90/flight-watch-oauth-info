Flight Watch

Flight Watch is a personal desktop application for monitoring flight prices. It is used by its author for private, non-commercial purposes.

What it does

  • Checks flight prices for routes the owner chooses.
  • Reads, with the owner's own authorization, only Gmail messages that carry one specific label set by the owner. It uses the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly) to recognize flight price alerts sent by airlines and similar sources.
  • Processes those price alerts locally on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not book flights and does not make payments.
  • It does not send, change, label, archive or delete email.
  • It is not a commercial service and has no customers.
